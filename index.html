About

Curious about what things are made of, and how they work.

I study Materials Science and Engineering at Lehigh University, with an expected graduation in May 2030. My current engineering work includes designing rocket aerostructures and running stability and flight simulations with the Lehigh University Rocketry Club.

At Drexel University’s ASM International Materials Camp, I explored ceramics, polymers, and nanomaterials, then designed a lightweight composite bridge. Outside engineering, yearbook editing, theatre crew, and lighting design have taught me to pair careful planning with clear communication and hands-on making.
